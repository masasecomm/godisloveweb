Why this exists

This directory is built on a simple conviction: God is love (1 John 4:8), and that love shows up in the small, daily work of raising children well , choosing a school where they will be formed, not merely taught. We believe a directory like this is a form of stewardship: helping families find a place that fits their child, their values, and their hopes for the years ahead.

Independent schools operate outside the government system and set their own admissions, staffing and curricula, which is why the offering varies so widely from one campus to the next , some follow the South African CAPS syllabus under IEB assessment, others teach Cambridge, the International Baccalaureate, or a national curriculum imported from abroad. Most are registered with a governing body such as ISASA, which sets standards for governance and teaching quality, but each school still runs independently: its own fees, its own admissions process, its own community. This register puts the basic facts for each school (location, curriculum, published fees, and approximate enrollment where available) in one searchable place, so a family can shortlist confidently before reaching out directly.
