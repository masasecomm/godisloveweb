About American International School of Johannesburg

The American International School of Johannesburg (AISJ) is a leading Pre-K through Grade 12 co-educational school. On two beautiful and spacious campus, with state-of-the-art facilities in Johannesburg and Pretoria, AISJ is a truly international community representing over 70 nationalities. We value and embrace the diversity of our students, faculty and staff, recognizing that it enriches our community and provides opportunities for connection and empathy as internationally minded individuals. AISJ offers an inclusive, nurturing and engaging learning environment, small class sizes, and a range of teaching strategies aimed at providing optimal learning and growth for all students. Our balanced program is based on the four pillars: academics, athletics and activities, arts, and service learning. AISJ offers an American curriculum as well as the International Baccalaureate Diploma Program (IBDP). AISJ’s Inclusion Services Program is expanding its support for neurodiverse learners.
