About Cambridge Academy

One of the countries premier academic institutions, Cambridge Academy has driven new ways of thinking since our 1998 founding. Today, Cambridge Academy is an intellectual destination that draws inspired scholars to our Emalahleni institution, keeping Cambridge Academy at the nexus of ideas that challenge and change the world. Cambridge Academy is dedicated to forming well-rounded, balanced young adults who will one day become strong, critical thinkers and an asset to society. ​In order to achieve this, we combine academics with sport, culture, firm and fair discipline, as well as praise where due.
