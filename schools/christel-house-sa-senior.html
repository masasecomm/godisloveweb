About Christel House SA Senior

Christel House South Africa is a non-profit school based in Ottery, Cape Town. It offers no-fee scholarships to students from some of Cape Town’s most under-resourced neighbourhoods and provide them with a character-based and career-focused education. Key aspects of Christel House’s model include poverty mitigation services in the form of daily transport to school and back, professional health care, nutritious meals, psychosocial counselling, family assistance and college and career support.
