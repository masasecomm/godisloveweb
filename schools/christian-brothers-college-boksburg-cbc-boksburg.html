About Christian Brothers' College Boksburg- CBC Boksburg

The boys’ school of choice on the East Rand of Johannesburg. Christian Brothers’ College Boksburg, fondly known as CBC Boksburg, forms part of the greater network of Edmund Rice schools in 20 countries across the globe .At the core of of the college, we are inspired by the teachings of Jesus, Gospel values and the spirit of Edmund Rice to educate young boys from year 000 to matric.Our purpose is to develop honest, enthusiastic, caring and competent young men, equipped to take on the challenges of life. Our aim is to be recognised as a leading Catholic School for boys. The boys’ school of choice on the East Rand of Johannesburg.
