About Clifton Preparatory School Nottingham Road

Nestled amongst farmlands in the town of Nottingham Road, in the KwaZulu-Natal Midlands, Clifton offers children opportunities that cannot be replicated in urban schools.A co-educational day and boarding school, Clifton is an inclusive inter-denominational Christian school, boasting high academic standards complemented by a full and varied extra-mural programme.
