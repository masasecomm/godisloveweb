About Darling College

Darling College is an independent school on the west coast of South Africa in the tranquil and peaceful village of Darling. The homely buildings and welcoming classrooms are framed by lovely, safe playgrounds.We consider ourselves as being one large family and believe in the active role of parents, whose commitment and involvement is an invaluable asset to the school.It would be a privilege to make a difference in your child’s life.
