About Deutsche Schule Durban (Primary)

Our primary school from Grade 1 to 7 boasts small classes, which allows for individual attention from our well trained and enthusiastic staff. Our bright and airy classrooms, surrounded by beautiful grounds provide a calm and nurturing learning environment.Combining the South African and German (Baden Württemberg) curricula, allows us to take advantage of the best of both worlds, all while drawing on local and international cultural traditions and modes of learning.
