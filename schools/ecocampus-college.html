About EcoCampus College

EcoCampus College is a small co-educational high school with a wonderful location overlooking the Sandpan. Priding ourselves on creating a nurturing environment for the students in our care, our focus is on the holistic development of South Africa's next generation of leaders.With small class sizes and dedicated staff members, EcoCampus College can be the stepping stone your child needs to achieve the future of their dreams.
