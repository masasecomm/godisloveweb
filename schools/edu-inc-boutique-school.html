About Edu Inc Boutique School

Edu Inc is a mainstream private school in Fourways with a 100% pass rate that enrols 10 learners per class. Our small classes enable us to offer hybrid learning and allows us to create a relationship-based style of teaching. Edu Inc supports all extra curriculars and accomodates students with these endevours, such as SA colours. Our teachers motivate our students and uplift the spirit of the school creating a productive and positive environment, where students can be their best selves.
