About English School Community Of Luanda Angola (E.S.C.O.L.A.)

E.S.C.O.L.A. is a small International School with a big heart. We cater for students from PP3 to Grade 9 in a classroom environment. Students continue at E.S.C.O.L.A. for their final high school years online, while attending and remaining a part of E.S.C.O.L.A. Class sizes are limited to a maximum of 25 students. The school day starts at 08h00 for the high school students, at 08h30 for the seniors, and 09h00 for juniors. The school day ends between 15h30 and 16h00.
