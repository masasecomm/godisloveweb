About Get Ahead College

Get Ahead College is a low-fee, independent high school situated in Komani, roughly 180km from East London in the Eastern Cape. Our students are all black African youth, from local rural townships. We pride ourselves in ensuring our schools live up to our mission of making “every child feel worthy and validated”. We are transitioning to the IEB starting with Grade 10 this year.
