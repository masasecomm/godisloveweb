About Glenridge Pre-Primary School

Glenridge pre-primary school is an English medium, multi-denominational, private school in Manor Gardens, Durban. Characterized by educational excellence, we guide and nurture individuals and install values of respect, tolerance and kindness for the diverse values, cultures and beliefs, which form part of the school community. In a safe a secure environment, individuals can flourish while learning to work and play together.
