About Hermitage House Independent School

At Hermitage House, we are dedicated to providing a warm, caring, co-educational environment where each student can thrive spiritually, academically, socially, culturally, and physically.We prioritise fostering enthusiasm, self-discipline, self-motivation, responsibility, and a strong Christian ethos, as well as other valuable qualities. These attributes, when taught at a young and receptive age, form the basis for lifelong success.
