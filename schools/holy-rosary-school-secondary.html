About Holy Rosary School (Secondary)

Holy Rosary started off as a single-building school on the Hurly farm outside Johannesburg in 1940. Over 80 years later, we stay true to our founding values: Empowering Women, and Service to Community.We are dedicated to helping each girl achieve her best. We do this by uncovering each child’s special talents and nurturing these talents. We want each child to be the best they can be.We have a reputation for strong academics, but this is only the visible layer of our school.
