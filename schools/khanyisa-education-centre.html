About Khanyisa Education Centre

Khanyisa Education Centre, founded in 1989, is located in the tranquil Limpopo Province bushveld, on the outskirts of Giyani. The Centre's 40-hectare campus nestles peacefully on the lower slopes of Man'ombe Nature Reserve. The Primary and Secondary schools are accommodated in beautifully designed buildings scattered amongst well-maintained gardens.At Khanyisa, we are prepared to go beyond the expected and bring children an education that they will remember long after the completion of their tuition. We achieve this feat by recruiting excellent, passionate and knowledgeable educators and ensuring that we remain abreast of the latest developments in technology. We provide up to date facilities, educational aids and equipment and implement the latest, proven learning and teaching techniques and education trends.
