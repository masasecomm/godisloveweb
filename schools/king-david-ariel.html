About King David Ariel

King David Ariel opened in January 2018 and is dedicated to excellence in remedial education. We support learners from Grades 1–7 who experience barriers to learning, offering small classes (two classes of 12 per grade), personalised learning, in-house therapies and specialist teachers. Children enjoy the full King David Schools experience, including Hebrew and Jewish Studies, davening, chaggim, sport and shared activities, within a warm Jewish environment. King David Ariel is ideal for learners needing individual support who can transition confidently into mainstream education. Admission is based on a multidisciplinary assessment review.
