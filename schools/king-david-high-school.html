About King David High School

King David High School Linksfield was established in 1955 as a community school and remains one of the leading Jewish Day Schools in South Africa and internationally. This reputation has been built on producing graduates who excel in both general and Jewish education.Our core philosophy is to nurture each learner into a true mensch: a person with self-respect and respect for others, empathy, humility and imagination. We aim to develop young people who think critically, communicate effectively, take accountability, and show genuine concern for the needs of others. King David students are encouraged to grow into holistically well-rounded individuals, equipped to meet life’s challenges spiritually, emotionally, academically, culturally and on the sports field.We strive to provide students with the widest possible range of opportunities to participate in and “own” their school, fostering a strong sense of belonging.
