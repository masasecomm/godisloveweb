About La Gratitude Pre-Primary School

Welcome to La Gratitude Pre-Primary School — a cherished cornerstone of the Pinelands community since the 1950s. Nestled in the tranquil, tree-lined suburb of Pinelands, Cape Town, our school offers a nurturing and faith-based environment where young children can learn, play, and grow with confidence.As the first official preschool established in Pinelands, La Gratitude has a proud heritage of providing quality early childhood education rooted in Christian values. We welcome families of all faiths into our inclusive community, where each child is celebrated and supported.Our small, private school comprises three intimate classes—Ducklings (Grade 000), Squirrels (Grade 00), and Bunnies (Grade R)—each guided by a qualified teacher and a dedicated teacher aide. This low teacher-to-child ratio ensures personalized attention and a warm, family-like atmosphere.
