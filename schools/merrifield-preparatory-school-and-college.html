About Merrifield Preparatory School and College

Our vision is to be a dynamic, globally competitive independent school rooted in the Eastern Cape. Merrifield strives to provide a rigorous academic environment of exceptional quality. Merrifield believes that educating the whole child includes sport, spirituality, culture, and community service. The school encourages students to explore our diverse offerings to develop their physical, emotional, and social well-being alongside their academic pursuits. Merrifield’s approach ensures that each student receives a well-rounded education. Merrifield recognises that each child is unique and provides a a curriculum that allows students to explore their interests and passions.
