About Michael Mount Waldorf School (Early Childhood)

At Michael Mount Waldorf School, we strive to nurture conscious individuals through dynamic education. Early Childhood includes Baby Care (4 months - 2 years), Play Group (2 -4 years), and Nursery School (4 - 6 years - Grade R). This is a nurturing and enriching space run on Waldorf principles. Daily activities focus on rhythm, language development through artistic expression, development of social skills, free play, storytelling, cooking and baking, singing, movement games, circle time and painting.
