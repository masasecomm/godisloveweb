About Midstream College Hoërskool | High School

At Midstream College we strive to build a learning environment that fosters confidence, participation and balance. Our academic and extra-curricular activities focus on providing various opportunities that cater for a multitude of interests. Our mission is to ensure well balanced learners who are equipped to enter the world as independent and confident young adults.Midstream College streef daarna om ‘n leeromgewing te skep wat selfvertroue, deelname en ‘n gebalanseerde lewe bevorder.
