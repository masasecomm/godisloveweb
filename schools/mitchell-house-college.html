About Mitchell House College

Our vibrant College in Polokwane is a forward-thinking high school that offers small classes, strong academic support, and a nurturing environment where learners grow into confident, capable young leaders. Since its first matric class in 2012, the College has focused on helping every learner realise and exceed their potential. It offers a balanced curriculum in Sciences, Commerce, Humanities, Arts, and Languages, supported by enrichment, pastoral care, and digital learning. Learners enjoy a vibrant co-curricular life with sport, cultural activities, service opportunities, and camps that build teamwork, resilience, and leadership. The College values excellence, integrity, respect, and personal growth, preparing learners for life beyond school. Its Scholarship Programme offers motivated, high-performing learners the chance to thrive, and applications are open to Grade 7 learners each year, wanting to apply for Grade 8 the following academic year.
