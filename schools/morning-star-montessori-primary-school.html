About Morning Star Montessori Primary School

Morning Star Montessori provides authentic Montessori education that nurtures each child’s full potential—academically, socially, and emotionally. Experienced, SAMA and SACE-registered teachers create a warm, stimulating environment where children develop independence, critical thinking, and a love of learning.High academic and moral standards are upheld while each child’s unique journey toward becoming a confident, capable, and well-rounded individual is supported.
