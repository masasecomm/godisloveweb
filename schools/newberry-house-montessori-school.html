About Newberry House Montessori School

Newberry House is located on the beautiful, iconic Lourensford Estate, on the outskirts of Somerset West in the Cape Winelands. Quality, international education in a healthy, sustainable environment providing real-life learning experiences are the three key pillars of the Newberry House offering.We offer world-class education for children age 18 months - 18 years through globally-recognised Montessori and Cambridge International programmes on an inspiring organic green campus.
