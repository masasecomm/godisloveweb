About Our Lady of Fatima Dominican Convent School (High School Grade 8-12)

We are a unique, all-girls Catholic-Christian school who focus on establishing strong foundations and preparedness for an unpredictable world.Our Lady of Fatima Dominican Convent School was founded for the glory of God and this must remain the cornerstone of all our academic, cultural and sporting endeavours. Religious Education, therefore, holds a prime position among our teaching subjects throughout the school and every girl is expected to attend these classes.Our motto “VERITAS”embraces the pursuit of TRUTH in all its forms. We strive to offer holistic development through quality education and the recognition of individual talents, affirmed in our Christ-centred family atmosphere.
