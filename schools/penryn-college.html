About Penryn College

Penryn is an independent, co-educational, school with a link to the Methodist Church situated in a tranquil, bushveld setting between Nelspruit and White River. Providing outstanding education, our warm, friendly and stimulating environment creates the opportunity for every child to thrive and develop into confident young adults with a passion for life.The breadth of diverse international perspectives, faiths, ethnicities and backgrounds cultivates a campus rooted in empathy and respect. Instilling a keen appreciation and awareness of self.
