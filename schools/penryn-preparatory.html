About Penryn Preparatory

True to the ethos of our brothers and sisters at the College, we find ways to centre learning on a global approach, without abandoning what is relevant and dear within the African context.We are all about creating a positive, energetic learning experience, but within the natural and unpretentious environment of the Lowveld. Our serene setting is the perfect playground for fostering an inquiry-based ethos, with a focus on teamwork, problem-solving and self-awareness.
