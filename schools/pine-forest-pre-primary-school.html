About Pine Forest Pre-Primary School

Pine Forest is a Christian school established in 1997, registered with the Gauteng Department of Education and a member of ISASA.We welcome babies from 3 months and cater for children up until Grade 00, offering a guided curriculum to prepare learners for Primary School. Pine Forest also provides a range of extra-mural activities and access to learning and development support professionals.
