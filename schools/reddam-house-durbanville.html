About Reddam House Durbanville

Reddam House Durbanville offers a co-educational, non-denominational, premium, private school environment recognizing, nurturing and developing the individuality of each student placed in our care and encouraging students to maximize their potential. Our school offers a dual curriculum, offering both Cambridge Assessment International and IEB education.
