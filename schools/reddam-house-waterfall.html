About Reddam House Waterfall

Reddam House Waterfall is a premium private school in Waterfall, Midrand. A definitive expression of excellence, the school boasts beautiful, lush, greenbelt views, within the secure estate bordering the Jukskei River; featuring beautiful walking and mountain bike trails, play parks, and picnic areas. This tranquil setting is conducive to discovery and learning.
