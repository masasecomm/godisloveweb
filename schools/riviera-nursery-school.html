About Riviera Nursery School

Riviera Nursery School offers a nurturing and stimulating environment for our young children.We pride ourselves on running an exciting, fun, play based learning programme.Our qualified teachers prepare inspiring activities and opportunities for continual age-appropriate social, emotional, cognitive and physical development.We believe nursery school is a partnership between the home and school. Parents, teachers and learners all work together for the benefit of the child.Our values stem from our belief that each child is unique and deserves every opportunity to reach their maximum potential in a dynamic and loving environment.We strive to ensure that our children leave us with an inherent love for learning; being competent, confident and well prepared for their future.
