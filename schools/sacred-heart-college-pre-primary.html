About Sacred Heart College (Pre-Primary)

Our aim is to instil a deep and natural love of learning that will stay with our children forever.We offer a pre-primary school experience for boys and girls from babies to Grade R.Our approach guides children to discover meaning and to encounter their world through exploration and play. The rich learning environment provides opportunities for children to learn through play, both indoors and outdoors in the gardens. The playground encourages children to extend their physical prowess and develop body confidence.The children engage with age-appropriate climbing equipment and play with sand and water to develop sensory skills.
