About Sentinel Kalumbila

From the inception, it was the vision of the founders of Sentinel Kalumbila to empower all who pass through it with an education which will open their minds and ready them for opportunities which will undoubtedly come their way.Sentinel Kalumbila inculcates a culture of lifelong learning which will translate itself into an enlightened society capable of dealing with whatever challenges come its way.
