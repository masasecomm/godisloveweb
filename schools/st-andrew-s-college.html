About St Andrew's College

Founded in 1855 St Andrew’s College in Makhanda (Grahamstown) is an innovative independent boys' full-time boarding school offering IEB and Cambridge A Levels. St Andrew's College is highly regarded for excellence in all spheres of education and our unique co-educational model with DSG. Our rich history and traditions are paired with adaptability and forward-thinking.
