About St Benedict's College

St Benedict's College is the final phase at St Benedict's and it is at this level where the foundations laid at St Benedict's Junior Preparatory and the Prep school become evident. Offering a holisting offering with a focus on our faith, St Benedict's College produces excellent results across Academics, Culture and Sports, making it the ideal environment for boys who are driven. Our purpose is to equip boys to become global citizens with integrity... A light in the world.
