About St Francis College

St Francis College is a member of ISASA (Independent Schools Association of Southern Africa). It is an English medium, co-educational, multi-cultural pre-primary and primary school with a Christian ethos. The school is located on Boschkloof Farm in the St Francis Bay area. It offers a personalized, caring, disciplined and environmentally aware education as well as a varied sport and cultural program.To provide a happy, caring, disciplined and multi-cultural environment, committed to high educational standards, incorporating traditional values and life skills, which will:Provide for the intellectual, emotional, cultural, physical and spiritual needs of pre-school and primary school childrenDevelop their full individual potential, enquiring minds and a love for learning and the environment, andEquip them for their role as valued members of society.
