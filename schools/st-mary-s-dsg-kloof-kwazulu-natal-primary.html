About St Mary's DSG Kloof, KwaZulu-Natal (Primary)

St Mary's is an independent, all-girls, Christian school, based in the heart of Kloof, Durban in KwaZulu-Natal. Since 1906, our experienced, nurturing teachers have cultivated and instilled curiosity and a love of learning in girls with a major focus on their holistic development.
