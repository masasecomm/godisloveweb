About St Patrick's CBC, Kimberley

St Patrick’s CBC is a prestigious School with a multicultural and socially diverse pupil population.​We are a Catholic School offering schooling from Age 1 to Grade 12, situated in the heart of Kimberley, in the Northern Cape of South Africa.Offering boarding from Grade 7 to 12. We prepare pupils with diverse backgrounds and aptitudes for a life of learning, leadership, service and personal achievement true to our history and the charitable intentions of our founder, blessed Edmund Rice.
