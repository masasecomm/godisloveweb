About Stedin College and Primary School

Stedin College and Primary School is an affordable, co-educational, independent school based in Walmer, Gqeberha, Port Elizabeth. It provides Christian-based education from Grades R - 12 using the CAPS and writes the National Senior Certificate in Grade 12. The school offers a holistic programme with a growing number of extramural activities to ensure all-round development. The motto is Onwards and Upwards which reflects the ethos of a school that is striving for excellence and quality education.
