About Summerhill School (Nursery)

Summerhill Nursery School is a loving and nurturing environment for children and parents alike! We prioritise the holistic development of each child, focusing on their social, emotional, language, sensory, motor, cognitive, and creative growth. With small classes and a tranquil, secure setting, we provide personal attention and a safe space for your child to thrive. Our comprehensive program includes a gross motor skills program, fully equipped living classroom, sports and games, swimming, soccer, educational shows, workshops, and music. We believe in fostering independent thinking, creative expression, technological awareness, and an appreciation for the natural environment and its creatures. Our diverse curriculum combines creative work, formal learning, music, computer lessons, and physical activities. We ensure a well-rounded development for your child. Our aim is to create well-prepared, well-rounded children who are ready to excel in Grade 1 and beyond.
