About Tomorrow's People College

Located in Faerie Glen, Pretoria East, our Pre-Primary School, Primary and High School is an English Medium school that provides children with a firm academic and social footing. Through the Foundation Phase (Grades 1 to 3), Intermediate Phase (Grades 4 to 6), Senior Phase (Grades 7 to 9), and FET Phase (Grades 10 – 12) we apply quality education and authentic character development. We believe in helping all children to develop to their full potential. Give your child the best possible preparation for the future. Besides Mainstream Classes, our Remedial and Special Needs Classes accommodate learners who may be facing learning difficulties. Our Remedial and Special Needs (LSEN) classes help learners with specific learning difficulties to smoothly integrate into our wider school environment
