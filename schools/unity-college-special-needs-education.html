About Unity College (Special Needs Education)

Wheelchair-friendly, (3-24Y). High-quality education to equip pupils with skills to be integrated members of society. Barriers to Learning: low vision, cochlea implants, epilepsy, MVA’s, birth trauma, genetic disorders, dyslexia, medical conditions & general developmental delays. Qualified Educators, Speech, Occupational Therapist Assistant, & Music Therapist on site. IDPs, DCAPS curriculum, 10/class, & more
