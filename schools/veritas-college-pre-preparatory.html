About Veritas College (Pre-Preparatory)

Veritas Pre-Preparatory is open to children from the age of 2 years to 6 years old. By providing a solid foundation from which to grow and learning on the long standing traditions of our school, the Pre-Prep is an integral part of the College as we feed the Preparatory and Senior Schools. Many of our top achievers start their schooling in our Pre-Prep.
