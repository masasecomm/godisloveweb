About Vuleka School Assisted Learning

Non-profit Christian Education in Johannesburg offering a leading private school network with high-quality education at competitive fees.Adapted CAPS curriculum for Special Learning NeedsWe aim to encapsulate every child’s strengths and promote them while developing coping strategies to overcome areas of weaknesses. Our goal is to bridge the identified gaps in social, emotional, physical, spiritual, and academic needs necessary to develop the child as a whole. We strive to foster their self-esteem to help them achieve their full potential. By imparting our values of being safe, respectful, and responsible, we nurture our children to become contributing and accepted members of society.
